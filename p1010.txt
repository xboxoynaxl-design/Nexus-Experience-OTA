Installation Instructions:
Flash madteam's CWM recovery https://sourceforge.net/projects/jbromgtp1010bk2/files/madteam-cwm-p1010-kpi.tar/download using Odin3 on PDA slot.
Insert a SD Card of atleast 16GB (32GB and above recommended)
Enter CWM Recovery, wipe cache, and dalvik cache (do NOT wipe data if that's where your ROM is)
Make sure to mount the SD Card, and backup all data on it, as everything will be wiped and formatted
After wiping proceed to flash the ROM (do NOT put the file on SD Card!!! Rather put it on the Internal Storage!!!), process might take sometime over 10 minutes.
Reboot the system and enjoy! First boot should take somewhat over 20 minutes
